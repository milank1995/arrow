## Full description (max 4000)

Arrow Away is a relaxing arrow puzzle game. Tap each arrow off the board in the right order until the whole shape is clear. It takes seconds to learn, it gets cleverer the further you go, and it plays fully offline.

WHAT IS ARROW AWAY?
Arrow Away is a free logic puzzle game for Android in which every level is a picture-shaped arrow maze. An arrow can only fly out when nothing blocks its way, so each tap is a small decision: which arrow is free right now, and which one will it set free next? Clear the board and the shape is solved.

HOW TO PLAY
• Find an arrow with an open path to the edge of the board.
• Tap it and watch it slide away.
• A blocked arrow bounces back and costs a heart, so plan before you tap.
• Stuck? Pinch to zoom, turn on the grid, or use a hint.
• Send every arrow away to complete the shape.

WHY PLAYERS ENJOY IT
✦ Shape mazes: boards drawn as hearts, swans, dolphins, sunflowers, lions and many more.
✦ A new puzzle every day: the Daily Challenge calendar, win streaks and trophies.
✦ Fresh levels are added regularly, so there is always another maze to solve.
✦ Calm by design: no timers and no rush. Play at your own pace.
✦ Beginner friendly: free hints on your first levels and a gentle difficulty curve.
✦ Two calm board styles: warm paper or clean light.
✦ Satisfying sounds and light haptic feedback, each of which you can turn off.
✦ Works offline: no Wi-Fi or mobile data needed, which is perfect for travel.

GREAT FOR
Short breaks, commutes and flights, winding down before sleep, and keeping your mind sharp with a daily logic puzzle.

QUESTIONS PLAYERS ASK
Is Arrow Away free?
Yes. This arrow puzzle game is free to download and play, and it is supported by ads.

Can I play without internet?
Yes. All puzzles, including the Daily Challenge, can be played offline.

How does the Daily Challenge work?
A new puzzle unlocks every day. Solve it on consecutive days to grow your streak, and open the calendar to play days you missed.

Is it difficult?
The first levels teach the rules step by step. Later boards are bigger and trickier, and hints are always there when you need a nudge.

Who makes Arrow Away?
Arrow Away is made by Number Game Studio, the developer of NumSlide and Number Flip.

Solve the arrow maze, clear the shape and give your brain a calm minute. Download Arrow Away today.
